How the score is calculated

Four components, each measured independently and combined by a fixed formula — never a model guessing a number.

Two different questions. The ATS Parse Score asks whether a parser can read your document at all. The Role Fit Score asks how well your experience matches a specific role in today's market. Parse score is one input to fit score — 20% of its weight — not a separate total added on top.

Real applicant tracking systems are proprietary and vary by vendor and by how each employer configures them. We don't claim to reproduce any specific one — these checks reflect common, well-documented parsing behaviour.

40%
Keyword coverage

How many of the skills real job postings ask for actually appear in your resume.

Weighted by how often each skill shows up across sampled postings for this role — not counted equally.

25%
Semantic fit

Whether your resume's wording actually matches what each requirement is asking for.

Compared sentence-by-sentence against real requirement text from sampled postings, not judged as one block.

20%
Format compliance

Whether an ATS parser can actually read your resume correctly.

Nine deterministic checks — tables, columns, contact info, dates, bullets, and more.

15%
Experience alignment

Whether your years of experience and education line up with what the market expects.

Compared against the median experience level found in live postings for this role.

score = 0.40·keyword + 0.25·semantic + 0.20·format + 0.15·experience
